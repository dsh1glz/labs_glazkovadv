About us

A small studio with a simple belief: great design should feel effortless.

We're a team of designers and developers who've spent the last six years helping startups and small businesses turn ideas into products people actually want to use. We started Aurora in 2019 after years of watching good ideas get buried under bloated agencies, endless revision cycles, and design that looked nice but solved nothing.

We work differently. Small team, direct communication, no account managers between you and the people doing the work. You talk to the designer. You see progress weekly. You get files you can actually use.

We care about craft — but we care more about outcomes. A beautiful interface that doesn't convert isn't a win. A brand that looks great but confuses customers isn't either. Everything we make is measured against one question: does it work?

We don't sell pixels. We sell the confidence that your product will work — and look like it deserves to.

Mira Halvorsen FOUNDER
